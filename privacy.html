Your data

Privacy, in plain language.

Portfolio data stays local in this release

Accounts, holdings, balances, tax inputs, and plans are processed in your browser and saved to the JSON file you choose. The current app has no portfolio-upload service. Your browser may remember a reference to a file you authorize so you can reopen it.

Public-site analytics

The hosted site also uses Google Analytics (GA4) to measure visits, new and returning browsers, and engagement. Google Analytics uses cookies and sends usage information to Google. Browser identifiers estimate visitors; they do not identify unique people reliably across devices. Google privacy policy.

The hosted site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics when enabled to understand page visits, referral sources, and page performance. The pricing page has a distinct path so its visits can help us understand interest. Page visits are approximate signals, not verified sign-ups or email sends. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not track clicks on our email links.

We do not add portfolio contents, filenames, account names, email addresses, or form values to analytics. We measure app screens, setup progress, file open/save outcomes, check-ins, report actions, and requests for paid-feature information using fixed event labels. Demo activity is labeled separately. We do not configure session replay or advertising personalization. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not use analytics cookies, local storage, or visitor fingerprinting. Network requests still reach Cloudflare to deliver this service.

Offline copy

The standalone app download contains no analytics script and works offline. Opening a link to our website or choosing to send us email leaves that offline experience.

Email and future cloud features

Get more information links open a draft in your own email app. Nothing is sent automatically. If you send an email, we receive your address and the message you choose to send so we can respond. Do not include financial documents. Cloud saving and reminders are planned features; their data handling and terms will be explained before you opt in.
